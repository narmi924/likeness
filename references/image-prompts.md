# Image prompts

Fill the brackets and give each attached image a role. Keep the chosen original in the project's references folder.

## Character or object turnaround

“Create orthographic front, right and back views of [subject] at the same scale, either in one aligned sheet or as three coordinated images. Keep the entire subject in frame, including feet and the top of the head. Use a neutral gray background, even soft lighting and an unchanging pose. Preserve [silhouette, proportions, costume and asymmetry] from [design reference]. Align the floor, eyes and shoulders across views, with no perspective distortion. Use the approved front view as the reference for the remaining views.”

Save as `concept-turnaround.png`, or use `concept-front.png`, `concept-right.png` and `concept-back.png`.

## Base color

“Create a square [1024 or higher] pixel base-color map for [material]. Use even color, without shadow, highlights, perspective or baked lighting. [Tile seamlessly across all four edges / follow the attached named UV layout exactly]. Keep [motif and colors] at [size or placement].”

Save as `suit-albedo.png`.

## Illustration or emblem

“Create one isolated [motif] with real transparent alpha outside the silhouette. Center the complete motif, leave [padding] on all sides, and preserve [line weight, proportions and colors] from the attached artwork. Export RGBA PNG.”

Save as `emblem.png`. Inspect the alpha before applying the image.
