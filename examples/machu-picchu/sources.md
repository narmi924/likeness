# Sources

## Used in the scene

- Site outlines and the city gate: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. The approach follows [way 629542954](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/629542954) into [way 844863624](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/844863624) at the [city gate](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/874540069). [Copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
- Elevation: Mapzen Terrain Tiles (Terrarium), AWS Open Data, a 3.4 km square centred on 13.16450° S, 72.54560° W. [Terrain Tiles](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/)
- `photos/city-gate.jpg`, the gate's jambs, lintel and paving: McKay Savage, CC BY 2.0. [Photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Peru_-_Machu_Picchu_068_-_city_gate_(7183500885).jpg)
- `ridge-at-dusk.jpg`, the summit, the city ridge and the evening light: Martin St-Amant, Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 3.0. Segmented, colour-adjusted and projected onto the terrain; the adapted images in `build/` are under the [same licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). [Photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:80_-_Machu_Picchu_-_Juin_2009_-_edit.2.jpg)
- `valley-1.jpg` and `valley-2.jpg`, the forested outer valley: Brian Jeffery Beggerly, CC BY 2.0. [Panorama of Machu Picchu](https://www.flickr.com/photos/beggs/49472067753/), [Machu Picchu panorama](https://www.flickr.com/photos/beggs/49472065043/)
- Relief of the far city: inferred from the St-Amant photograph with [Depth Anything V2 Small](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2), Apache 2.0. Relative depth only, not surveyed heights.
- Ground, granite, rubble and sky: Poly Haven, CC0. [Leafy Grass](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass), [Gravel Floor 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel_floor_02), [Rock Boulder Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry), [Rock Wall 08](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_wall_08), [Lichen Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/lichen_rock), [Namaqualand Stones 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/namaqualand_stones_01), [Evening Road 01 Pure Sky](https://polyhaven.com/a/evening_road_01_puresky)
